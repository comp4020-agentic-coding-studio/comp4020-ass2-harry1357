The course runs two lectures and ten working sessions. This is the first, and
it exists to make one argument: a battle system is a small, finite, mostly
deterministic thing, and the reason it doesn't feel that way is presentation.

Forty minutes, three parts.

## What we mean by a system

An animation, a damage number and a turn order are three views of the same
handful of rules. We take one fight apart on the screen and put the rules on
the board — nothing you couldn't do yourself with a pause button and patience,
which is the point.

## Why spreadsheets

Because a spreadsheet forces you to say what depends on what, and because a
model you can step forward one tick at a time is a model you can be wrong in
public with. Every week of this course ends in one.

The three small items and the design document, with dates. Bring questions
