Two lectures, both in the first fortnight. The first argues that a battle
system is a readable object; the second works Final Fantasy X's counter model
through on the board, and carries the slides.

After that the course is sessions. Watching somebody model a system is the
second-best way to learn it, and ten weeks is enough time for the best way.
