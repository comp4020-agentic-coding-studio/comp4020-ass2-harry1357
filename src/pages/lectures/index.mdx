This course teaches in sessions, with a spreadsheet open. A lecture exists only
for the two things a spreadsheet can't do: state the claim, and derive the
counter model once on a board where everyone watches the same term move.

That's lecture 1 and lecture 2. After lecture 2 the room has spreadsheets open
and stays that way for ten weeks.
