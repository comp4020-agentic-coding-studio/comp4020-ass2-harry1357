# Policies and support

One page so the assessment briefs don't each carry their own version of the
rules and slowly disagree with one another.

## Late work

Five percent of the item's total off per working day, up to five days, after
which it isn't marked. The fight logs are the exception: they're a running
artefact, so a missing week costs that week and nothing else.

## Extensions

Ask before the deadline if you can, after it if you couldn't. Marisol grants
them and doesn't require a story — a sentence saying what happened and what
date you want is enough. An extension on the design document past the last
teaching day needs a reason the university recognises, because marking has to
finish.

## Using an AI assistant

You may use one. Say so, and say what for.

Every submission carries a short note naming what you used and where. "Wrote
the spreadsheet formulas with an assistant, checked them against the recording
myself" is a complete declaration. A submission with no note is a claim that
you used nothing.

This isn't a hoop. The three small items are marked on method, and a method you
can't describe isn't one — if an assistant fitted your curve and you can't say
what shape it assumed, the teardown scores badly whether or not you declared it.
The design document is the same: a stranger has to be able to implement it, and
nothing about that gets easier if you didn't write it.

## Your own work, and other people's

Work you hand in is yours. Numbers, formulas and mechanics documented on a wiki
or in a published guide are not yours and don't need to be — cite where you got
them, the way the week pages do.

The week 12 session has you implement a classmate's design document. Their
document stays theirs; what you hand in is your implementation and your
question log.

## Access and adjustments

If something about how the course runs is in your way, say so early and it will
be changed if it can be. The lab sessions have the least flexibility because
they're scheduled around a room; everything else, including how work is handed
in, is negotiable.

Recordings of every fight the exercises use are provided, so no exercise
requires owning or playing a particular game.

## Getting help

Idris first for anything practical — a spreadsheet that misbehaves, a formula
that won't fit, a recording you can't find. Marisol for anything about the
design document, marking or dates.

If a question has gone unanswered for two working days, send it again. That's
not rudeness, it's the agreed escalation.
